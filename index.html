QA Master Blueprint: English Audio Record

An audit-ready 3-Layer QA Matrix designed to standardize speech dataset reviews—eliminating subjectivity across compliance gates, linguistic accuracy, and acoustic signal integrity.

Layer 1: Compliance & Safety Gate

0–30 Pts

Audits PII redaction, recording consent, target locale adherence, and policy boundaries. Any fatal breach immediately triggers an Auto-Reject regardless of linguistic or audio scores.

Layer 2: Linguistic Quality

0–40 Pts

Evaluates verbatim script accuracy, omission/substitution rates, speaker diarization, natural intelligibility, and fluency without penalizing acceptable regional accents.

Layer 3: Audio Technical Quality

0–30 Pts

Checks signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), clipping, room echo, microphone consistency, silence boundaries, and clean JSON metadata formatting.
